About Balaji C

Balaji C is the Founder & CEO of Thaalam and VYIOM Digital Solutions. Holding a degree in BE - Mechatronics Engineering, he combines technical precision with a vision for strategic entrepreneurship.

Driven by a passion to solve systemic challenges for growing businesses, Balaji created Thaalam and VYIOM Digital Solutions to serve as bridges between emerging enterprises and the essential tools, digital technologies, expertise, and connections they need to scale.

" Thaalam is built on the belief that every business deserves access to technology, knowledge, connections, and opportunities. "

Many entrepreneurs and businesses have great ideas, products, and services, but they often struggle with finding the right technology, business knowledge, and connections needed to grow.

MSMEs, SMEs, and startups face challenges in adopting modern technologies, building a strong digital presence, understanding business strategies, and connecting with the right professionals and opportunities. These challenges can slow down growth and make it difficult for businesses to compete in a rapidly changing world.

The founder identified the need for a platform that brings everything businesses need into one connected ecosystem. Thaalam was created to help businesses become smarter by providing access to technology solutions, business knowledge, professional connections, and growth opportunities.
