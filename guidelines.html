1 Building a Professional Business Community

Thaalam aims to create a trusted and professional environment where businesses and professionals can learn, connect, collaborate, and grow together.

2 Professional Communication

Members are encouraged to maintain professional communication, share valuable business knowledge, exchange ideas, and build meaningful relationships.

Zero Tolerance: Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, abusive behaviour, or unprofessional communication are not allowed.

3 Genuine Information & Authentic Profiles

Businesses and professionals should provide genuine information, represent themselves honestly, and keep their profiles updated. Creating fake listings, misleading information, or misrepresenting business expertise is strictly prohibited.

4 Valuable Knowledge & Growth Insights

Thaalam encourages members to share business experiences, industry knowledge, technology insights, entrepreneurship discussions, and growth strategies. Spam content, misleading promotions, irrelevant advertisements, and low-quality contributions should be avoided.

5 Respect Other Businesses & Collaboration

Members should respect other businesses, avoid unwanted marketing practices, and build relationships through professional communication and collaboration.

6 Technology Solutions & Business Services

Businesses interested in technology services can request solutions through Thaalam. Services such as software development, websites, AI solutions, automation, digital marketing, and other technology solutions will follow separate service agreements.

7 Trust, Safety & Content Removal

Thaalam may remove content, restrict profiles, or take necessary action against activities involving fraud, fake information, abuse, illegal activities, harmful content, or behaviour that affects the safety and trust of the community.

8 The Thaalam Community

Thaalam is a business community that helps businesses connect, learn, grow, and access technology solutions.
