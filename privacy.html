1 Introduction

Welcome to Thaalam. Thaalam is a business community platform designed to help entrepreneurs, businesses, professionals, and organizations connect, learn, share business insights, discover opportunities, and access business solutions. Thaalam collects information from businesses and professionals to create business visibility, enable networking, and improve community experiences. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and manage your information when you use our platform.

2 Information We Collect

Business Submissions: When businesses submit information to Thaalam, we may collect business-related details such as business name, business category, industry type, company description, products and services, logo, business images, location details, website links, social media profiles, contact details, and founder or company representative information.

Professional Registration: When professionals register or create a professional profile, we may collect information such as name, professional designation, industry, skills, expertise, experience, company association, professional profile details, LinkedIn profile links, portfolio links, and contact information if provided.

Community Participation: When members participate in the Thaalam community, we may collect information related to their community activities, including business or professional identity, discussions, comments, feedback, and other contributions shared through the platform.

Solution & IT Service Requests: If you request business or technology solutions from Thaalam or our associated IT services, we may collect information such as your name, company details, email address, phone number, business requirements, and project information to understand your needs and provide suitable solutions.

3 How We Use Your Information

Thaalam uses collected information to:

  • Create business and professional visibility
  • Connect businesses and professionals
  • Support networking opportunities
  • Share business knowledge and insights
  • Improve community experiences
  • Understand business requirements
  • Provide requested business and technology solutions
  • Communicate relevant updates
  • Maintain a trusted professional ecosystem

4 Publicly Available Information

Business listings and professional profiles created on Thaalam may be visible to visitors and community members. Publicly available information may include business names, industry details, company descriptions, products and services, logos, images, website links, social media links, professional roles, skills, experience, and other information submitted by businesses and professionals.

Please Note: Businesses and professionals should only submit information that they are comfortable making publicly available.

5 Payments and Services

Thaalam community participation, business listings, and professional listings are provided without any listing fees. We do not collect payments for joining the community, creating business listings, or creating professional profiles.

Payments may apply only when businesses request specific solutions or services such as website development, software development, digital transformation, artificial intelligence solutions, automation solutions, digital marketing services, technology consulting, or other professional services provided by Thaalam or its technology partners. Any paid services will be managed through separate service agreements and pricing discussions.

6 Information Sharing

Thaalam does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when required to provide requested services, support business networking activities, work with authorized service providers, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

7 Data Accuracy

Businesses and professionals are responsible for ensuring that the information they submit is accurate, complete, and updated. Thaalam may review, verify, update, or remove information that is incorrect, misleading, outdated, or violates platform guidelines.

8 Data Removal Request

Businesses and professionals may request profile updates, information correction, or removal of their listing by contacting Thaalam.

Contact Email: support@thaalam.com
