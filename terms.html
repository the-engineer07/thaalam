1 About Thaalam

Thaalam is a business community platform that helps businesses, entrepreneurs, professionals, and organizations connect with each other, share business knowledge, discover opportunities, build professional visibility, and access business and technology solutions.

2 Community Access

Thaalam provides community access to encourage business networking, knowledge sharing, and professional collaboration. Business listings and professional listings are currently provided without any registration or listing fees.

3 Business Listings

Businesses may submit information to showcase their company profile, products and services, achievements, online presence, and contact information. By submitting business information, businesses provide permission for Thaalam to display and organize their profile information within the platform to improve visibility and discovery.

4 Professional Listings

Professionals may create profiles to showcase their expertise, skills, experience, professional background, and industry knowledge. Professionals are responsible for ensuring that their submitted information represents their actual professional identity.

5 Business Solutions and Paid Services

Thaalam and its technology partners may provide paid business solutions including website development, software development, digital transformation services, AI solutions, automation solutions, digital marketing, and technology consulting. Pricing, scope, timelines, and agreements for these services will be discussed separately with businesses.

6 User Responsibilities

Members using Thaalam agree to provide accurate information, respect other businesses and professionals, share valuable and professional content, and use the platform responsibly. Members are responsible for the information and content they submit.

7 Prohibited Activities

Members must not:

  • Create fake business or professional profiles
  • Provide false information
  • Misuse business contact details
  • Send spam
  • Share harmful or illegal content
  • Copy content without permission
  • Impersonate another person or organization
  • Use the platform for fraudulent activities

8 Business Connections Disclaimer

Thaalam helps businesses discover, connect, and build professional relationships. However, Thaalam does not guarantee business partnerships, sales results, revenue growth, customer acquisition, employment opportunities, or success from any connection made through the platform. Businesses and professionals should independently evaluate opportunities and relationships.

9 Content Ownership

Businesses and professionals retain ownership of the information and content they submit. By submitting information to Thaalam, you grant permission for the platform to display, organize, and promote your profile and business information for community and discovery purposes.

10 Removal Rights

Thaalam reserves the right to review, restrict, modify, or remove content and profiles that violate these terms, reduce community trust, contain misleading information, or negatively affect other members.
